This is hardware that has been verified. It is not a complete catalog.

## CPUs

- Xeon E-23xxG
- Xeon D-1700, D-2700, D-1800, D-2800. BIOS updates may be infrequent.
- Xeon Max. BIOS updates may be infrequent.
- Xeon 3rd Gen Scalable. Supported. Multi-socket needs extra configuration.
- Xeon 4th Gen Scalable. Supported.
- Xeon 5th Gen Scalable. Supported.

## Motherboards

These boards expose SGX and DCAP in BIOS:

- Supermicro X11SCM-F. Systems SYS-5019C-M and SYS-5019C-MR.
- Supermicro X11SCW-F. System SYS-5019C-WR.
- Supermicro X11SCZ-F.
- Supermicro X11SSL-F.
- Dell R240. BIOS 2.14.1.
- Dell R350. BIOS 1.7.3.
- HP DL20 G10. BIOS 1.80_07-20-2023.
- HP DL20 G10+.
- ASUS RS100-E10-PI2. BIOS 5601.

See [Requirements](/operators/requirements) for OS, memory, storage, and device nodes.
